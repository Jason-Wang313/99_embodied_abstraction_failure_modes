# Novelty Boundary Map

## Crowded Territory

- Physics-aware task-and-motion planning.
- Grounded geometric TAMP.
- Semantic model-predictive planning.
- LLM-TAMP failure reasoning.
- Runtime monitoring and replanning.
- VLA agents with motion planning.
- Neuro-symbolic predicates.
- Active relational state abstraction.
- Corrective planning with language models.

## Boundary Tested

The potentially novel boundary was a risk-bounded abstraction-failure audit that detects erased mechanics and decides whether to refine predicates, query mechanics, switch to TAMP, monitor/replan, recover, abstain, or proceed.

## What The v5 Expanded Audit Found

The boundary is not strong enough. The proposed v5 method has worse hard success, mechanical violation, damage, regret, and robust utility than `physics_aware_tamp`. It also fails diagnostic, ablation, maximum-stress, fixed-risk, scope, and external-validation gates.

## Boundary Decision

Novelty remains an idea seed and a useful negative audit, not a submission-ready contribution.
