# Novelty Decision

Chosen thesis: risk-bounded mechanics-aware auditing for embodied abstraction failures.

Decision after v5 expanded evidence: KILL_ARCHIVE.

Reason: the claimed novelty is not empirically decisive. The 2026-06-22 audit shows that the full v5 mechanism loses badly to `physics_aware_tamp` on success, safety, regret, and robust utility. The ablation suite also shows that removing or replacing core pieces matches or beats full v5 on at least one decisive objective.

Future revival would need evidence that the full audit, not merely grounded geometric planning, physics-aware TAMP, semantic MPC, monitoring, or failure reasoning, causes robust embodied-planning gains under real or high-fidelity deployment conditions.
